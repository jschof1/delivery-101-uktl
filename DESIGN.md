# UKTL Client Proposal Design

## Theme

Dark, practical and operational. The proposal is reviewed on desktop or mobile as a decision document. It should feel calm, serious and easy to scan.

## Colors

- Page: `#151515`
- Deep section: `#101010`
- Elevated surface: `#222222`
- Elevated surface strong: `#272727`
- Primary accent: `#fbc02d`
- Primary text: `#f7f7f5`
- Muted text: `#b9b9b3`
- Divider: `#3a3a36`
- Positive status: `#69b578`

## Typography

Use Segoe UI, Roboto, Helvetica and Arial. Headings are compact, heavy and practical. Body copy is readable, short and limited in width. Uppercase labels are reserved for small operational cues.

## Components

- Compact utility header with UKTL identity and a single primary CTA.
- Two-column hero with client partnership mark and one decisive recommendation.
- Numbered operating modules, status pills and structured diagrams.
- Elevated charcoal cards with subtle borders and 12px to 18px radii.
- Yellow reserved for emphasis, active states, connectors and primary actions.
- Client imagery used selectively inside framed, neutral surfaces.

## Layout

Use a 1000px to 1100px content area, 24px desktop gutters and 18px mobile gutters. Alternate dense decision sections with visual system diagrams, proof and video. Stack cleanly below tablet widths.

## Client Branding

Living Bathrooms' logo and imagery provide context, but do not replace UKTL yellow and charcoal as the proposal shell.
