# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Living Bathrooms decision-makers reviewing a commercial recommendation from UK Trade Leads. They need to understand the system, investment, limits and next step within five to eight minutes.

## Product Purpose

Present a concise, evidence-led recommendation for connecting paid acquisition, CRM, follow-up and revenue attribution. Success means the client understands that UK Trade Leads is proposing a measurable operating system, not simply advertising management.

## Brand Personality

Practical, assured, informed.

## Anti-references

Not a glossy agency brochure, generic presentation template, orange lifestyle campaign, oversized marketing report or unsubstantiated forecast.

## Design Principles

- UK Trade Leads remains the dominant visual system.
- Client branding appears as a considered partnership signal.
- Operational diagrams and precise labels do more work than paragraphs.
- Commercial terms and uncertainty remain obvious.
- Every section should help the decision move forward.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain strong contrast, visible focus states, readable labels, reduced-motion support, descriptive alt text and a clean 390px mobile layout.
